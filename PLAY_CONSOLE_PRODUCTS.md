# Google Play one-time products

Create these exact product IDs in Play Console.

| Product ID | Suggested price | Game reward |
|---|---:|---:|
| `coins_50000` | $5.02 | 50,000 coins |
| `coins_110000` | $10.02 | 110,000 coins |
| `coins_180000` | $15.02 | 180,000 coins |
| `coins_260000` | $20.02 | 260,000 coins |
| `coins_350000` | $25.02 | 350,000 coins |
| `coins_450000` | $30.02 | 450,000 coins |
| `coins_570000` | $35.02 | 570,000 coins |
| `coins_710000` | $40.02 | 710,000 coins |
| `coins_860000` | $45.02 | 860,000 coins |
| `coins_1050000` | $50.02 | 1,050,000 coins |
| `remove_ads` | Choose price | Removes interstitial ads |

Coin packs are consumable. `remove_ads` is non-consumable and can be restored.

The actual storefront price shown inside the app comes from Google Play when the product is active. Eligible Play Points coupons are handled by Google Play checkout.
