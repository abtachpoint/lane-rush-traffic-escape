Privacy Policy for Lane Rush: Traffic Escape

Last updated: September 17, 2026

No account required: Lane Rush: Traffic Escape does not require users to create an account or sign in.

Local Game Data

The game stores progress locally on the device, including coins, best score, selected and unlocked cars, missions, daily reward status, settings, and purchase-processing markers. Local progress may be lost if the app is uninstalled or app storage is cleared.

Advertising

The game may display optional rewarded advertisements and limited interstitial advertisements using Google Mobile Ads. Advertising services may process device information, approximate location derived from network information, advertising identifiers, diagnostics, and ad interaction data according to their own policies and applicable consent choices.

In-App Purchases

Optional digital purchases are processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive full payment-card details. Consumable coin packs are granted in the game after a successful Google Play purchase. A non-consumable Remove Ads purchase may be restored through Google Play.

Children and Families

The developer should configure the app's target audience, advertising treatment, content rating, and Play Console declarations accurately before release. Advertising and data handling must follow the rules that apply to the selected audience and countries.

Data Security

The game does not operate its own login database in this version. Platform services such as Google Play and Google Mobile Ads use their own security practices.

Changes

This policy may be updated when features, advertising, analytics, purchases, or data practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on the Google Play listing for Lane Rush: Traffic Escape.
