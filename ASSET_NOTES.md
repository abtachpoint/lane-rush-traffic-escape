# Included visual/audio assets

The project includes original generated game assets made for this starter project:
- 12 top-down car PNGs
- Coin and power-up icons
- 1024x1024 app artwork
- 1024x500 feature graphic draft
- Splash artwork draft
- Original simple WAV sound effects and music loop

Files are under:
- `assets/images/cars/`
- `assets/images/ui/`
- `assets/audio/`

The Android launcher mipmaps are already generated from the app artwork.
